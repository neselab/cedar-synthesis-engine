Viewing Knowledge Base Pages

A user may view a page only if they hold at least one of the reader, contributor, reviewer, or space administrator roles and are the page’s author. The user’s clearance and the page’s classification must each be 0, 1, 2, or 3, and the user’s clearance must be at least the page’s classification.

The page must be in draft status, have external sharing enabled, and carry the “restricted” label. These are the only permissions: viewing a page is allowed when all these requirements are met, and no other actions are permitted.
