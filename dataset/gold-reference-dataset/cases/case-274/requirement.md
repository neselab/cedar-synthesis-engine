Inpatient Prescription Permissions

A user may countersign a prescription if they hold the Attending Physician role, are the prescription’s recorded prescriber, and have used multi-factor authentication.

A user may dispense a prescription if they hold the pharmacist role and the prescription’s recorded status is countersigned.

A user may write a prescription if they hold the Attending Physician role, are not the prescription’s patient, and have used multi-factor authentication.

These are the only permissions: each activity is allowed exactly when its stated requirements are met. Users may hold multiple roles. These permissions neither change records nor establish an order for performing the activities.
