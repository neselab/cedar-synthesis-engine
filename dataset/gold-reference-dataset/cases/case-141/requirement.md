Wire Transfer Release

A user may release a wire transfer only if the user holds the treasury officer role, is not the transfer’s verifier, and has used multi-factor authentication. The transfer must have verified status, its amount must be at least $0.00, and its beneficiary must be included among the transfer account’s allowed beneficiaries. Amounts in the supplied records are stored in whole US cents.

If the transfer is marked high risk, the high risk review must also be completed. This review requirement applies only to high-risk transfers; all other requirements apply in either case.

These are the only permissions: release is permitted when all applicable requirements above are met, and no other actions are authorized.
