Inpatient Medical Record Viewing

A user may view an inpatient medical record only if they hold at least one of the attending physician, resident, or nurse roles. The user’s clearance level and the record’s sensitivity level must each be 0, 1, or 2, and the user’s clearance must be at least the record’s sensitivity.

The record must be open, the request’s current-shift indicator must be true, the user must be included in the record’s care team, and the user’s ward must match the record’s ward. Together, these conditions are sufficient for viewing; these are the only permissions granted.
