Course Module Viewing

A user may view a course module only if the user holds at least one of the instructor of record, teaching assistant, learner, or coordinator roles and is also the module’s recorded instructor of record. Role membership alone does not establish that relationship. The user’s completed level and the module’s prerequisite level must each be 0, 1, or 2, and the completed level must be at least the prerequisite level. The module must have released status and must not be locked.

These are the only permissions granted; no other activity is permitted under this requirement.
