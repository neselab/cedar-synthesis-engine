Issuing Order Refunds

A user may issue a refund only if they hold the service rep or manager role and the refund relates to an order with shipped status and loyalty tier 2. The refund amount, the order’s item total, and the order’s tax amount must each be at least $0.00. Amounts in the supplied records are stored in whole US cents.

If the order’s item total plus tax exceeds $100.00, or the refund amount exceeds $50.00, the user issuing the refund must differ from the user who placed the order. At or below both thresholds, this separation requirement does not apply; all other requirements still apply.

These are the only permissions to issue a refund; no other actions are authorized by this requirement.
