Folio Adjustment Approval and Posting

These are the only permissions for users to approve or post folio adjustments, and each permission requires all of its stated conditions. They are evaluated using current recorded facts; they neither change records nor require an order between approval and posting. Amounts in the supplied records are stored in whole US cents.

Approval is permitted for an Accounting Clerk or Revenue Manager when the folio’s adjustment amount and folio balance are each at least $0.00 and its dispute category includes “chargeback”. The user must differ from the folio’s posting author unless a GM override has been declared and the user is a Revenue Manager. That exception waives only the requirement to differ from the posting author; all other approval requirements still apply.

Posting is permitted for a Front Desk Agent or Night auditor when the user’s adjustment authority level and the folio’s guest segment are each one of 0, 1, 2, or 3, the adjustment amount and original charge are each at least $0.00, and the folio’s dispute category includes “comp”. Only when the adjustment amount exceeds $500.00 must the user’s adjustment authority level also be at least the folio’s guest segment.
