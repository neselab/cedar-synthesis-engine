Immigration Case Adjudication

A user may adjudicate an immigration case only if the user holds the supervisory officer role, has not recused themselves from that case, and is not the case’s applicant. Holding the adjudication officer role alone is insufficient; a supervisory officer need not also hold that role.

The request must be made on the government network. The request’s current time must be at or after its scheduled adjudication start time and strictly before its scheduled adjudication end time. Times are in UTC.

These are the only permissions: adjudication is permitted when all these requirements are met, and no other activities are authorized by this requirement.
