Document Viewing Access

A user may view a document only if they hold at least one of the attorney, paralegal, or records manager roles, are the document’s author, and do not list the document’s matter among their conflicted matters. These are the only permissions for access under this requirement, and all applicable conditions below must also be met.

The request’s current date and time must be at or after its scheduled start and strictly before its scheduled end. Times are in UTC. If the document is privileged, privileged access must also be authorized; this additional requirement does not apply to a nonprivileged document.
