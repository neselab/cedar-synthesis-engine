Content Watching Access

A user may watch content only if they hold the subscriber role, their subscription tier and the content’s required tier are each 0, 1, or 2, and their subscription tier is at least the content’s required tier. The content’s embargo must end at or before the request’s current time, its labels must include “restricted,” the device must be trusted, and the content’s allowed regions must include the user’s region.

These are the only permissions: watching content is allowed when all these requirements are met, and no other activities are authorized by this requirement.
