Scoring Grant Applications

A user may score a grant application only if the user holds the reviewer role, the application is under review, its requested amount is at least $0.00, and the request is made on the government network. The request’s current time must be at or after its scheduled scoring start time and strictly before its scheduled scoring end time, using UTC.

If the requested amount exceeds $100,000.00, the large award review must also be completed. Amounts in the supplied records are stored in whole US cents.

These are the only permissions: scoring is allowed when all applicable requirements above are met, and no other action is permitted under this requirement.
