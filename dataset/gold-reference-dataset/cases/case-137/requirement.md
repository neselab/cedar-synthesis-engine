Wire Transfer Approval

A user may approve a wire transfer only if they hold the verifier or treasury officer role and have an approval tier of 0, 1, or 2. The transfer must have an amount tier of 0, 1, or 2, and both its amount and fee must be at least $0.00. Amounts in the supplied records are stored in whole US cents.

If the amount plus the fee exceeds $75,000.00, or the amount alone exceeds $50,000.00, the approving user must not be the transfer’s initiator. This separation requirement applies only when at least one of those thresholds is exceeded; all other requirements still apply.

The transfer’s beneficiary must be among the account’s allowed beneficiaries, and the request must be made on the secure network. These are the only permissions: approval is permitted when all applicable requirements above are met, and no other action is permitted.
