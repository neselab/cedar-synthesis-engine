Page Review Access

A user may review a page only if the user holds the reviewer or space administrator role, the page is in the in_review status, and its labels include “restricted.” The user’s membership space must be the same as, or descend from, the page’s space, and the request must be made on the corporate network.

The request’s current time must be at or after its scheduled review start time and strictly before its scheduled review end time. All times are UTC. These conditions together are the only permissions granted: a review is permitted when all are met, and no other actions are permitted under this requirement.
