Publishing Marketplace Listings

A user may publish a listing only if they hold the seller role, own the listing, and have a verified tier of 0, 1, 2, or 3. The listing must be in draft status, have a category tier of 1, 2, or 3, and have a price of at least $0.00. Its risk labels must include “high_risk”, and its category must begin with “STANDARD-”, followed by any sequence of characters, including none.

If the listing’s price exceeds $500.00, the host application must confirm that the additional review is complete. This review requirement does not apply at or below $500.00; all other requirements still apply. Amounts in the supplied records are stored in whole US cents.

These are the only permissions: publishing is allowed exactly when all applicable requirements above are met, and no other actions are permitted.
