Folio Adjustment Approval

A user may approve a folio adjustment only if they hold the accounting clerk or revenue manager role and are not the folio’s posting author. Both the adjustment amount and the folio balance must be at least $0.00. The folio’s dispute categories must include “billing_error,” the dispute must be verified, and the folio code must begin with “PROPERTY-A-”.

If the adjustment amount exceeds $25.00, large credit approval must also have been recorded. This additional requirement does not apply to amounts of exactly $25.00 or less; all other requirements still apply. Amounts in the supplied records are stored in whole US cents.

These are the only permissions: approval is allowed when all applicable requirements above are met, and no other activity is authorized by this requirement.
