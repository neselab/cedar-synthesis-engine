Page Review Access

A user may review a page only if they hold the reviewer or space administrator role, are not the page’s author, and are a member of the page’s space. The page must have the in_review status and the external label.

The request’s current time must be at or after its scheduled review start and strictly before its scheduled review end. These times are in UTC.

These are the only permissions: page review is allowed when all of these requirements are met, and no other actions are permitted under this requirement.
