Scoring Grant Applications

A user may score an application only if they hold the reviewer role, the application's requested amount is at least $0.00, the application is not among the user's recorded conflicted applications, and the user is not the application's applicant. The request must be on the government network, and its current time must be at or after its scheduled scoring start and strictly before its scheduled scoring end. All times are UTC.

If the requested amount exceeds $500,000.00, the large award review must also be completed. Amounts in the supplied records are stored in whole US cents.

These are the only permissions: scoring is permitted if and only if all applicable requirements above are met, and no other action is permitted.
