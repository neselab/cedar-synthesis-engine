Course Module Viewing

A user may view a course module only if they hold at least one of the instructor of record, teaching assistant, learner, or coordinator roles and are the module’s recorded instructor of record. The user’s completed level and the module’s prerequisite level must each be 0, 1, or 2, and the completed level must be at least the prerequisite level. The module’s release time must be at or before the request’s current time, measured in UTC.

The module must have released status unless the request’s preview window flag is true and the user both holds the instructor of record role and is the module’s recorded instructor of record. This exception waives only the released-status requirement; all other requirements, including the release-time requirement, still apply.

These are the only permissions: viewing is allowed when all applicable requirements above are met, and no other activity is permitted under this requirement.
