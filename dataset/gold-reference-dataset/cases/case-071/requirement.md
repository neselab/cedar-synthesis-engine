Interview Feedback Submission

A user may submit interview feedback only if they hold the Interviewer role, belong to the application’s hiring team, and are not the user recorded as having referred the application. The interview feedback must have scheduled status, and the request must be made on the corporate network.

The request’s current time must be at or after its scheduled start time and strictly before its scheduled end time. All times are UTC.

These are the only permissions: submission is allowed when all these requirements are met, and no other actions are authorized.
