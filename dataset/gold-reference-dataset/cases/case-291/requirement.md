Inpatient Prescription Access

A user may countersign a prescription if and only if they hold the Attending Physician role, are the prescription’s recorded prescriber, and have used multi-factor authentication.

A user may dispense a prescription if and only if they hold the pharmacist role and the prescription’s recorded status is countersigned.

A user may write a prescription if and only if they hold the Attending Physician role and are not the prescription’s patient.

These are the only permissions. Users may hold multiple roles, but role membership does not establish that they are the recorded prescriber. These permissions do not change prescription records or establish an execution order between activities.
