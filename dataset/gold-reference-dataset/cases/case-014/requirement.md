Campaign Budget Approval and Launch Access

These are the only permissions: users may approve campaign budgets or launch campaigns only under the respective conditions below. Permissions are determined from current recorded facts; they neither change records nor require an order between these activities. Amounts in the supplied records are stored in whole US cents.

Budget approval

A user with the campaign director or account manager role may approve a campaign budget if the budget and contingency are each at least $0.00 and the budget tier is 0, 1, or 2. If the budget plus contingency exceeds $15,000.00, the approving user must be someone other than the campaign’s preparer, unless the request is marked as an emergency launch and the user has the campaign director role. This exception waives only the requirement to be someone other than the preparer; all other approval requirements still apply.

Campaign launch

A user with the account manager or campaign director role may launch a campaign only if its status is approved, the user is the campaign’s advertiser, and its risk labels include “restricted.” Role membership alone does not establish that the user is the advertiser.
