Listing Publication Permission

A user may publish a listing only if they hold the seller role, own the listing, and have used multi-factor authentication. The user's verified tier must be 0, 1, 2, or 3, the listing's category tier must be 1, 2, or 3, and the user's verified tier must be at least the listing's category tier. The listing must carry the risk label “restricted” and have a price of at least $0.00.

For a listing priced above $2,000.00, the host application must also confirm completion of the additional review. This review requirement does not apply at or below $2,000.00; all other requirements still apply. Amounts in the supplied records are stored in whole US cents.

These are the only permissions: publishing a listing is allowed if and only if all applicable requirements above are met, and no other actions are permitted.
