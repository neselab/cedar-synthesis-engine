Patent Application Access

A user may access a patent application only if the user holds at least one of the patent attorney, docket clerk, or examiner roles and is the application's recorded prosecuting attorney. Both the user's clearance and the application's confidentiality must be one of 0, 1, or 2, and the user's clearance must be at least the application's confidentiality.

The application must have filed status, and the request must be on the firm network. The request's current date and time must be at or after its scheduled start and strictly before its scheduled end, with all times interpreted in UTC. These are the only permissions granted; no other actions are permitted.
