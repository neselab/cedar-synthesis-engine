Viewing Job Applications

A user may view a job application only if they hold at least one of the recruiter, hiring manager, interviewer, or coordinator roles, the application is in screening status, its requisition code begins with “ENG-” (with any suffix, including an empty suffix), and the request is made on the corporate network.

The user’s personally identifiable information clearance and the application’s personally identifiable information sensitivity must each be 0, 1, or 2. The user’s clearance must be at least the application’s sensitivity unless the request has executive approval and the user holds at least one of the roles listed above. This exception waives only the clearance-to-sensitivity comparison; all other requirements, including the permitted clearance and sensitivity levels, still apply.

These are the only permissions granted, and no other actions are permitted under this requirement.
