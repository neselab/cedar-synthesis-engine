Lease Review Access

A user may review a lease only if they hold the broker role and the request is made on the brokerage network. Holding the transaction coordinator role without the broker role is not sufficient. The lease’s segment must include “commercial,” its monthly rent must be at least $0.00, its square footage must be nonnegative, and its term must be between 1 and 120 months, inclusive. Amounts in the supplied records are stored in whole US cents.

The user must not be the lease’s listing agent unless a court order has been declared and the user holds the broker role. This exception waives only the restriction on reviewing a lease as its listing agent; all other requirements remain in effect.

These are the only permissions: lease review is allowed only under all applicable conditions above, and no other actions are permitted.
