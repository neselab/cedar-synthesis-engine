Campaign Budget Approval

A user may approve a campaign budget only if they hold the campaign director or account manager role, the campaign is in review status, and the user has used multi-factor authentication. The campaign’s budget and contingency must each be at least $0.00, and its budget tier must be 0, 1, or 2. Amounts in the supplied records are stored in whole US cents.

When the budget exceeds $10,000.00, the approving user must not be the campaign’s preparer. When the budget tier is 2, the approving user must hold the campaign director role. All other requirements continue to apply in both cases.

These are the only permissions: campaign budget approval is permitted exactly when all applicable requirements above are met; no other actions are permitted.
