Review of Advertising Creative

A user may review advertising creative only if they hold the brand safety reviewer role, have a reviewer level of 0, 1, or 2, and have used multi-factor authentication. The creative must have a risk rating of 0, 1, or 2, have a status of rejected, and include “restricted” among its content tags. The reviewer must not be the creative’s submitter.

These conditions together define the only permission granted; no other actions are permitted by this requirement.
