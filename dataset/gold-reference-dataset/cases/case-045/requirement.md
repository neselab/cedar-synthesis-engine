Document Editing Access

A user may edit a document only if they hold the author role, the editor role, or both; own the document; and are included in its share list. The document must carry the “restricted” label, and the request must be made on the corporate network.

The user’s clearance and the document’s classification must each be one of 0, 1, 2, or 3, and the user’s clearance must be at least the document’s classification. These are the only permissions: editing is allowed when all these requirements are met, and no other actions are permitted under this requirement.
