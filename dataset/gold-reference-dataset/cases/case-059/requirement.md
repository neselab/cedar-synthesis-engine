Reading Classified Documents

A user may read a document only if the user holds the analyst or officer role, the user's clearance and the document's classification are each one of 0, 1, 2, or 3, and the user's clearance is at least the document's classification. The document must have released status and include CHARLIE among its compartments, and the request must be made on the intranet.

These conditions together are sufficient and are the only permissions granted; no other actions are permitted.
