Classified Document Reading

A user may read a document only if they hold the analyst role, the officer role, or both. The user's clearance and the document's classification must each be one of 0, 1, 2, or 3, and the user's clearance must be at least the document's classification.

The document's compartments must include BRAVO, and the user's compartments must include every compartment of the document. The request must be recorded as being on the intranet.

These are the only permissions granted by this requirement; no other activities are authorized.
