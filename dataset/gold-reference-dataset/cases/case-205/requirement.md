Viewing Inpatient Medical Records

A user may view an inpatient medical record only if the user holds the attending physician role, is included in that record’s care team, and has used multi-factor authentication. Resident or nurse membership alone is not sufficient; users may hold multiple roles.

The user’s clearance level and the record’s sensitivity level must each be 0, 1, or 2, and the user’s clearance must be at least the record’s sensitivity. Together, these conditions are the only permissions for viewing inpatient medical records; no other actions are permitted under this requirement.
