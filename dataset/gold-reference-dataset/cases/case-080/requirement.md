Complimentary Benefit Access

A user may apply a complimentary benefit to a comp authorization record only if they hold the front desk agent or guest services host role, their comp authority level and the record’s guest tier are each one of 0, 1, 2, or 3, and their authority level is at least the guest tier. The record’s comp value must be at least $0.00, its blackout flag must be false, and the request must be made on the property network.

A user may authorize a complimentary benefit on a comp authorization record only if they hold the shift manager or front office manager role. The record must have a comp value of at least $0.00, an item count of at least 0, and a status of requested. Its comp types must include casino, and its perks must include beverage_credit.

Amounts in the supplied records are stored in whole US cents. These are the only permissions, and each is granted only when all of its stated requirements are met. These permissions neither change records nor require either activity to precede the other.
