Advertising Creative Review

A user may review advertising creative only if they hold the brand safety reviewer role, have used multi-factor authentication, and are not the creative’s submitter. The user’s reviewer level and the creative’s risk rating must each be 0, 1, or 2, and the reviewer level must be at least the risk rating. The creative must have pending status and include “advertising” among its content tags.

These are the only permissions: review is allowed when all these requirements are met, and no other activity is authorized by this requirement.
