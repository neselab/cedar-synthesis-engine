Underwriting Exception Approval and Policy Binding

These are the only permissions: a user may perform either activity on an application only when all requirements for that activity below are met. Assess permission using current recorded facts; these permissions neither change records nor require an order between the activities. Amounts in the supplied records are stored in whole US cents.

Approving an underwriting exception

A user may approve an underwriting exception only if they are the application's assigned underwriter and hold at least one of the Senior underwriter, Underwriting Manager, or Chief underwriter roles. The application's premium must be at least $0.00. If its coverage lines include “controlled_liability”, the coverage exception review must be completed. If its premium exceeds $1,000.00, the user must also hold the Underwriting Manager or Chief underwriter role.

Binding an insurance policy

A user may bind an insurance policy on an application only if they hold at least one of the underwriter, Senior underwriter, or Underwriting Manager roles. The user's authority level and the application's risk tier must each be one of 0, 1, 2, or 3, and the authority level must be at least the risk tier. The application's premium must be at least $0.00, its coverage lines must include “excess_surplus”, and its product code must begin with the exact text “CONTROLLED-”, followed by any sequence of characters, including none.
