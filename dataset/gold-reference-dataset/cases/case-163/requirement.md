Document Editing Access

A user may edit a document only if they hold the author or editor role, their clearance and the document’s classification are each one of 0, 1, 2, or 3, and their clearance is at least the document’s classification. The document must be in draft status, must not be version locked, and must carry the “review_required” label. The request must be made on the corporate network.

These are the only permissions: editing is permitted when all these requirements are met, and no other actions are permitted under this requirement.
