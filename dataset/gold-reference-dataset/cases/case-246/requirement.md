Lease Review Access

A user may review a lease only if they hold the broker or transaction coordinator role and the lease includes the “high_value” segment. The lease must have monthly rent of at least $0.00, nonnegative square footage, and a term of 1 through 120 months, inclusive. Amounts in the supplied records are stored in whole US cents.

For a lease exceeding 20,000 square feet, the reviewing user must not be its listing agent. If monthly rent exceeds $10,000.00, the reviewing user must hold the broker role. If the lease includes a “title_clearance” contingency, the contingency review must have been completed.

These are the only permissions: lease review is permitted when all applicable requirements above are met, and no other activities are authorized.
