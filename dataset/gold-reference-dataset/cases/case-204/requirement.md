Award Approval

A user may approve an award only if they hold the program officer or grants officer role, their approval authority is 0, 1, or 2, and the award is pending. The award amount must be at least $0.00, its application's funding tier must be 0, 1, or 2, and the request must be made on the government network. Amounts in the supplied records are stored in whole US cents.

For an award exceeding $100,000.00, the user's approval authority must also be at least the application's funding tier, unless an emergency has been declared and the user holds the program officer role. This exception waives only the authority-to-tier comparison; all other requirements still apply.

These are the only permissions: award approval is permitted exactly under these conditions, and no other action is permitted.
