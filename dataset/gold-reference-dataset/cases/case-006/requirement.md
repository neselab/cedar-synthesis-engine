Underwriting Exception Approval and Policy Binding

These are the only permissions for users to approve underwriting exceptions or bind policies, and both apply to applications under the conditions below. Amounts in the supplied records are stored in whole US cents. These permissions do not change records or require either activity to occur before the other.

A user may approve an underwriting exception only if they hold the underwriting manager or chief underwriter role, the application has referred status, its segment includes commercial, and its premium is at least $0.00. The senior underwriter role alone is not sufficient.

A user may bind a policy only if they hold at least one of the underwriter, senior underwriter, or underwriting manager roles. The user's authority level and the application's risk tier must each be one of 0, 1, 2, or 3, and the authority level must be at least the risk tier. The application's premium must be at least $0.00, and its product code must start with “EXCESS-”, followed by any sequence of characters, including none. If the premium exceeds $100,000.00, the host application must confirm completion of the additional review; this review requirement does not apply at or below $100,000.00. All other binding requirements apply regardless of premium.
