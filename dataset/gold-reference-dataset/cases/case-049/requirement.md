Zone Schedule Overrides

A user may override a zone schedule only if the user has the facility manager role and is the zone’s recorded controller. The user may also hold other roles; the zone controller role alone does not qualify. The user’s control tier and the zone’s criticality must each be 0, 1, or 2, and the user’s control tier must be at least the zone’s criticality. The zone must be active, and the request must indicate that occupancy is detected.

These are the only permissions: a schedule override is permitted when all these requirements are met, and no other activity is authorized by this requirement.
