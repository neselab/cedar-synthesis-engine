Underwriting Exception Approval and Policy Binding

These are the only permissions: a user may approve an underwriting exception or bind an insurance policy for an application only when all requirements for that activity below are met. Amounts in the supplied records are stored in whole US cents.

A user may approve an underwriting exception only if they hold the senior underwriter, underwriting manager, or chief underwriter role, are the application's assigned underwriter, and the application's premium is at least $0.00. If the application includes commercial_property coverage, the coverage exception review must be completed. If its premium exceeds $10,000.00, the user must hold the underwriting manager or chief underwriter role.

A user may bind an insurance policy only if they hold the underwriter, senior underwriter, or underwriting manager role and their authority level is 0, 1, 2, or 3. The application's premium must be at least $0.00, its risk tier must be 0, 1, 2, or 3, and its status must be quoted. Its segments must include commercial, and its coverage lines must include controlled_liability.
