Content Watching Access

A user may watch content only if the user holds the subscriber role, both the user's subscription tier and the content's required tier are 0, 1, or 2, and the user's tier is at least the content's required tier.

The content's embargo must end at or before the request's current time, its labels must include “review_required,” and the device must be trusted. Times are in UTC.

These are the only permissions: watching content is allowed exactly when all these requirements are met, and no other actions are permitted.
