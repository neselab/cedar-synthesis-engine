Document Viewing Access

A user may view a document only if they hold at least one of the author, editor, viewer, or compliance officer roles. Both the user’s clearance and the document’s classification must be one of 0, 1, 2, or 3, and the user’s clearance must be at least the document’s classification.

The document must carry the “external” label, its share list must include the user, and the request must be made on the corporate network. These are the only permissions: viewing is allowed when all these conditions hold, and no other actions are permitted under this requirement.
