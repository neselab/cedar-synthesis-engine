Marketplace Payout Approval and Disbursement

A user may approve a payout only if they hold the finance approver role, are not the payout’s initiator, and have used multi-factor authentication. The payout amount must be at least $0.00, and its seller account’s KYC tier must be 2 or 3.

A user may disburse a payout only if they hold the payout agent role, are the payout’s initiator, and make the request on the trusted network. The payout amount must be at least $0.00, and its seller account’s KYC tier must be 1, 2, or 3.

Amounts in the supplied records are stored in whole US cents. These are the only permissions: each operation is permitted exactly when all of its stated requirements hold for a user and a payout, and no other operations are permitted. Users may hold multiple roles; role membership does not establish ownership. These permissions use current recorded facts and neither change payout state nor require an execution order between approval and disbursement.
